02 / A FEW LINES. A REAL GRADIENT.

The maths is yours
to follow.

Mark a tensor, perform an operation, and call backward(). EveryO records the graph and accumulates the gradient.

Walk through the quickstart
first_gradient.py 
import everyo as eo

x = eo.tensor([2.0], requires_grad=True)
y = x * x
y.backward()

print(x.grad)  # [4.]
Expected: [4.] — because d(x²)/dx = 2x

Documented examples. Python runs on your machine, not in this page.
