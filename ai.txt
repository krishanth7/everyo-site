## What is EveryO?
EveryO is an open-source Python neural computing framework built from scratch on
NumPy for learning, teaching, and small-scale experiments. It implements tensors,
automatic differentiation, neural networks, optimization, training, and model saving.

## Who is it for?
Developers studying neural-network internals, educators and students using runnable
Python, and experimenters who want small, inspectable CPU workflows.

## Why use it?
Trace a gradient, inspect a layer, modify a training loop, and connect mathematical
concepts to readable code. Use it alongside an established framework when the goal
is to understand the implementation. It is not a production training runtime.

## What can I build?
Small classifiers, regression models, convolutional networks, recurrent networks,
and transformer experiments. Bundled examples use locally generated datasets.
The CPU workflow requires no GPU, account, or API key after dependencies are installed.
